# 0319. A `Cow` is its enum, made owned by a clone of what it borrowed

Status: Accepted. Extends [0033](0033-enums-with-fields.md) and
[0172](0172-utf8-decoding.md); counted by
[0314](0314-std-data-structures.md).

Case: A ([0262](0262-when-rust-and-js-disagree.md)), but for a `Cow`
whose owned value isn't what it borrows in JS, D: refused.

## Context

ADR 0172 made a `Cow<str>` `{ TAG, _0 }` for `from_utf8_lossy`, and read
its text. Any other `Cow`, a `Cow<[T]>` say, couldn't be cloned, shown,
or read through `Deref`, and neither `to_mut()` nor `into_owned()` of one
was known: ADR 0314's count had `Cow` at none of its methods.

## Decision

**A `Cow` is its enum, `{ TAG, _0 }`, of a type whose borrowed and owned
values are one JS value: `to_mut()` and `into_owned()` make it owned by a
clone of what it borrowed, and the rest reads `_0`.**

| Rust | JS |
|---|---|
| `*cow`, `cow.len()`, `{:?}` | `cow._0`, and its borrowed type's `Debug` |
| `cow.into_owned()` | `cow.TAG === "Borrowed" ? <clone of cow._0> : cow._0`, or `cow._0` where a clone can't be told apart (ADR 0020) |
| `cow.to_mut()` | `if (cow.TAG === "Borrowed") { cow.TAG = "Owned"; cow._0 = <clone> }`, then `cow._0` |
| `cow.to_mut()` of text or a number | `$cowMut(cow)`, a handle on `_0` (ADR 0152) |
| `cow.clone()` | its variant's copy, as an enum's (ADR 0033) |

- **Which types**: the borrowed and owned types of std's `ToOwned`, `T`
  and `T`, `str` and `String`, `[T]` and `Vec<T>`, `Path` and `PathBuf`.
  Any other is refused, `a Cow<..> of a type with its own ToOwned`; a
  crate's own `ToOwned` is refused already.
- **A slice's clone is a new array**, `.slice()`, which the owned `Vec` may
  change; another's is its `Clone`'s.
- **An enum whose every variant is copied whole**, with no field to clone,
  is one `{ ...value }`, not a test of each variant: `Cow`'s and any
  enum's.
- **A path needs no clone**: it's text (ADR 0173), as a `String` is.

## Why

- **It's the same program**: what it borrowed is never changed through
  it, and what it owns is its own.
- **It's tested**: a corpus case runs each against native Rust, `Cow<str>`
  borrowed and owned, `to_mut().push_str`, a `Cow<[i32]>` changed through
  `to_mut()` with the array it borrowed printed after, `into_owned()` of a
  borrowed one pushed to, and a `Cow<Path>`; one a crate's own `ToOwned`
  refused. Mutations share the borrowed array, keep the borrowed tag,
  refuse the handle and a `Cow<Path>`, and share an enum's clone.
- `docs/std-coverage.txt`: `Cow` 2 of 2.
